The universe is enormous.

There are billions of galaxies, and each galaxy contains billions of stars. Many of those stars have planets.

So where is everybody?

## The basic question

The Fermi Paradox is the contradiction between the apparent likelihood of extraterrestrial civilizations existing and the complete lack of convincing evidence that we've encountered one.

If intelligent life is possible elsewhere, and the universe has had billions of years to produce it, shouldn't someone have contacted us by now?

## Possible explanations

Maybe intelligent life is incredibly rare.

Maybe civilizations destroy themselves before becoming advanced enough to communicate across the stars.

Maybe life is common, but intelligent life is not.

Maybe civilizations exist, but the distances between stars make communication practically impossible.

Or perhaps we're simply looking in the wrong way.

## The unsettling possibility

There is another possibility.

Maybe advanced civilizations are out there.

Maybe they know we're here.

And maybe they have no reason to talk to us.

The Fermi Paradox leads into the Drake Equation, the Great Filter, extraterrestrial civilizations, and some genuinely uncomfortable questions about humanity's future.
