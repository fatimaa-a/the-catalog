Black holes are some of the strangest objects in the known universe.

They aren't giant cosmic vacuum cleaners.

They're regions of spacetime where gravity becomes so extreme that once something crosses a certain boundary, it cannot escape.

## The event horizon

The boundary around a black hole is called the event horizon.

Cross it, and returning becomes impossible.

Not because the black hole is sucking everything inward like a drain, but because spacetime itself has become curved to such an extreme degree that every possible path leads deeper inside.

## What happens inside?

We don't completely know.

General relativity predicts a singularity at the center, where density and curvature become extreme.

But our current theories of physics break down under those conditions.

That means black holes may be pointing directly at something we don't yet understand about reality itself.

## And they get stranger

Black holes can collide.

They can merge.

They can bend light around themselves.

And they can slowly lose energy through Hawking radiation.

The deeper you go into black-hole physics, the less intuitive the universe becomes.
