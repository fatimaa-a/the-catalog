Ancient Rome lasted for centuries and became one of the most influential civilizations in history.<br>
It built roads, aqueducts, theatres, temples, legal systems, and cities across an enormous territory.<br>
But Rome wasn't exactly peaceful.

## An empire built on expansion

Rome began as a small city and gradually expanded across much of Europe, North Africa, and the Mediterranean.
Its armies were highly organized, disciplined, and extremely effective.
Roman roads connected distant parts of the empire, allowing soldiers, merchants, and information to travel much faster.

## Daily life

Roman society was wildly unequal.<br>
Emperors and wealthy families lived in enormous houses while enslaved people performed much of the labor that kept society running.
Public entertainment was also a huge part of Roman life.
The Colosseum hosted gladiatorial contests, animal hunts, executions, and other spectacles watched by enormous crowds.

## Why did Rome fall?

There wasn't one single moment when Rome simply disappeared.
The Western Roman Empire weakened through political instability, economic problems, military pressure, civil wars, and invasions.
The eastern half of the Roman world continued for another thousand years as what we now call the Byzantine Empire.
Rome didn't really vanish.<br>
Its language, laws, architecture, political ideas, and cultural influence survived long after the empire itself changed beyond recognition.
